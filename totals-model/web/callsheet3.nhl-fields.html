The simulation is the final decision on the full-game and first-period totals: the sheet's own projection is shown beside it, not scored. The chance shown is the sim's raw count; the worth is that chance shrunk by what the replay of 2025-26 found it worth, and the band reads the worth. The replay (1,312 games, the profiles built from the games before each date): the own view's full-game lean kept a quarter of itself against Pinnacle's close (the leans at 58% and up went 207-174; with the market's own price in the model the sim's weight was nil), its first-period lean 61% against DraftKings' 1.5 (the anchored view's 78%) and nothing over the first half of 2024-25 (631 games, no prior season behind the profiles), 36% pooled, and the book's price itself read sharper. The slate names each side's likely starter and fills his line, and the two lists above hold every goalie on each club with the line the sheet would score. A late change in net is the one thing a hockey market prices imperfectly, so check RotoWire's lineups (the goalie is marked confirmed or expected) or CBS's goalie depth chart, pick the goalie they name (his line and the Backup box follow), then tick Confirmed — only on a confirmed start, not an expected one. The next morning's grade file carries who actually started, and the record tile keeps the total's record split by whether the goalie on the card was the one who played: 42 of the first 150 goalie boxes were the other goalie's line. Backup in net: set from the pick when the goalie has under 30% of his club's starts, ten starts in; fix it if RotoWire or CBS says otherwise. With one backup in a game the total landed 0.18 under the close over two seasons (737 games, the under 53.7%), so one backup puts a tagged −0.15 on the line and the record tile keeps the under's record on these games; a backup whose own line reads cold takes −0.25 (182 such games landed 0.44 under, the under 57.5%). Two backups: nothing moves. With a backup in either net the goalie lines are not scored: in those games neither goalie's line had a direction against the close, while with two regular starters the lines ran the right way both seasons.
