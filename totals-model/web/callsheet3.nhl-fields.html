Daily Faceoff · starting goalies

The slate names each side's likely starter and fills his line. A late change in net is the one thing a hockey market prices imperfectly, so check Daily Faceoff, tick the boxes, and if the other goalie is starting, open the boxes below and type his line over the slate's (the slate's printout lists both). Backup in net: the slate ticks it when the named starter has under 30% of his club's starts, ten starts in; fix it if Daily Faceoff says otherwise. With one backup in a game the total landed 0.18 under the close over two seasons (737 games, the under 53.7%), so one backup puts a tagged −0.15 on the line and the record tile keeps the under's record on these games. Two backups: nothing moves. With a backup in either net the goalie lines are not scored: in those games neither goalie's line had a direction against the close, while with two regular starters the lines ran the right way both seasons.

Filled by the slate goalie lines, shots, special teams, form, rest — open to check or edit

A save percentage is shrunk toward the league by the shots behind it, so the two goalie boxes travel as a pair: both or neither, on each side. Shots for per game and the first-period last ten are shown, not scored, since the 7 Oct backtest (the shot lean ran the wrong way against two seasons of closes; the period's recent past said nothing). Special teams are shown, not scored too, since the detail pass: each side's power play and kill to date ran the wrong way against two seasons of closes. Shots, special teams and the last ten wait until a team has five games; until then the slate leaves them blank and the sheet is the market plus the goalies. Form is tagged and cannot buy a band; a back to back (0 days rest) is shown, not scored. The first-period last ten comes from the league ledger (Run NHL ledger.bat) and moves the first period the way the full game's last ten moves the total: weighted 0.8 against the market's 4, both sides or neither. Expected goals come from MoneyPuck's team file, all situations, per game, with that table's league mean beside them; all four or none, weighted 1.2 and tagged.
