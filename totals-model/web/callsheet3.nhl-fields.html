A save percentage is shrunk toward the league by the shots behind it, so the two goalie boxes travel as a pair: both or neither, on each side. Shots, special teams and the last ten wait until a team has five games; until then the slate leaves them blank and the sheet is the market plus the goalies. Special teams and form are tagged and cannot buy a band; a back to back (0 days rest) is shown, not scored.
