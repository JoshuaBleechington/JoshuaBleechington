A projection built this way is unbiased on every market over 2025-26 and misses by k × √projection, one sigma, k measured per market (1.85 points, 1.2 rebounds, 1.15 assists, 1.1 threes, 1.9 combined), a normal that held 69% within one sigma, read around the median (the mean shifted down a measured fraction of a sigma: a box-score stat skews right, and at the mean the over lands 46% of the time, 41% on threes, while the books post the median); that chance is blended with the book's own at 4 to 2, the market the spine as everywhere on this page.
