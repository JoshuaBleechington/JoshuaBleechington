Anchors on what the market is really on — the prices, not the rounded number — then adjusts for what today changes. Both books attack the total: MLB runs, WNBA points. Names a side on every card and grades itself once the games land.

The number prices matter more than the line

Outlier · games Covers odds Vegas Insider

Enter both prices. They are the most valuable thing on the board and the old model threw them away. A posted total is rounded to the half run; the prices are not. Over −120 with the under at +100 says fair sits well above the number that got posted, and the model solves for exactly how far.

Opened at is shown, never scored. The current line is the anchor, so a move is already inside it — subtracting it again would double-count. It is here because a big move against your side is worth seeing before you bet.

Starting pitchers scored as a differential

Innings behind each ERA. Fill them in and a short season gets pulled toward league average — a 5.24 in 22 innings is a reading with a ±1.7 error bar, not a fact. Leave them blank and the ERA goes in at face value, exactly as before.

Both arms or neither. These are scored as a gap from a league-average starter, not as a projection of runs. Two average starters move the line by exactly zero — that is arithmetic, not a calibration that came out right, and it is the fix for the permanent over-lean the previous model carried.

Same differential treatment, over the roughly 3.6 innings a pen covers. First-five existed partly to delete this half of the game; a full-game market cannot, and the bullpen is where a lot of nine-inning totals are actually decided.

Park & weather the one unpriced input

RotoWire · weather Statcast · park factors Action · public betting

Only the part of the wind blowing along the home-to-centre axis carries a ball. A reported compass bearing is almost never straight in or straight out — Outlier draws the park-relative diagram, so read it and pick quartering when the arrow crosses the field at a diagonal. It keeps cos(45°) of the speed. On a 14.9 mph ENE wind at Yankee Stadium that is the difference between a BET and no bet, because the 8 mph dead zone is taken off the resolved figure, not the raw one.

Wind is the only thing on this page the market prices imperfectly, because it changes after the number posts. Everything else here is published and already inside the line. Read the hourly row, never the card headline — “8 mph wind blowing in West Sacramento” is a card whose own hourly row says out.

Park factor scales the differentials and the wind, never the market anchor. The posted number already holds the park; counting it twice is the double count that put the old fourteen-input model behind the naked line.

The ticket/money split is shown and never scored, the same as the opening line. It used to move the number a flat 0.30 runs on a 20-point gap. Over 172 logged games that adjustment pointed the right way 27 of the 58 times it fired — 46.6%, and no threshold from 5 to 40 points beats a coin. The direction is documented, the size never was. Still worth entering: a 30-point gap against your side is a thing to see before you bet, and it now tells you instead of quietly moving the answer.

Pace how many possessions

stats.wnba.com · teams advanced StatMuse · pace & ratings

Use the PACE/40 column on stats.wnba.com, not PACE. WNBA games are 40 minutes, not 48, and the two columns differ by about a fifth. League average on that column is 80.59, which is the number the model is calibrated against.

Both constants have to come off the same table as your inputs. This shipped with 83.1 from a summary article while the paces being entered averaged 80.5, and every card carried one to two points of phantom under lean — the model called the under on everything, which is a stuck clock rather than an edge. basketball-reference says 79.3 for the same concept. None of them are wrong; they are different possession formulas and are not interchangeable.

Both teams or neither. Scored as a differential: two league-average paces move the total by exactly zero. That is arithmetic, not a calibration that came out right.

Efficiency points per 100 possessions

Each offence is scored against the defence it actually faces, not against the league. A 110 offence into a 98 defence is a different game from a 110 into a 112, and the average of the two ratings hides that.

All four or none. Same differential treatment — four league-average ratings move the total by zero.

OFFRTG and DEFRTG from the same table as the pace. League average is 107.5 on that basis. The check that the two constants share a possession denominator: mean OFFRTG 107.52 against mean DEFRTG 107.47 across the fifteen clubs. Every point scored is a point allowed, so those must agree — and that test failed on the numbers this shipped with.

Rest measured null · the weakest input here

WNBA · schedule

This is the one input here with a hand-picked size, and it is flagged as such. A back-to-back takes 2.0 off that team's offensive rating and one day's rest takes 1.0 — the direction is documented, the magnitude is a guess inherited from the older WNBA model. It is tagged so the corroboration gate can refuse a card that only it is carrying.

Two days is the baseline, so 2 or more moves nothing. This is also the input most likely to find you an under: tired legs score less.

Combined game totals — both teams' points added. Treated the same way MLB form is: an absolute rather than a differential, and tagged measured-null, so it cannot buy a band on its own.

The playoff box is a LABEL, not an adjustment. Playoff basketball is widely believed to be lower scoring, and that belief is exactly the kind of thing this project has been wrong about before. It moves the number by zero. It marks the row so the regular-season and playoff records can be compared from your own log, and if a gap shows up with enough games behind it, then it earns a coefficient.

Saving draft saved

Nothing is lost when you close the tab. What you are typing is written to this browser after every keystroke and comes back when you reopen. The card is stored the same way. Nothing to press.

Rescore re-runs every game on the card through the model as it stands today, from the inputs each row already stores. A row keeps the band it was given when you added it, so after a change to the model a card can hold calls the current model would not make — rows like that are marked. Finals and results are never touched.

Browser storage is not a vault. It does not follow you to your phone and it goes away if you clear site data. Take a backup file at the end of a night you care about — that is the copy that survives a new laptop.

Alternate lines chance of hitting

This is the one thing on the page that does not need the model to be right. A book prices its main line sharply — the single fact this project has actually established. It prices the alternate ladder off a template, and templates are coarse. Given the market's own fair number, recovered from the two main-line prices, the chance at every other rung is arithmetic on the same distribution. It uses the market estimate, never the blend.
