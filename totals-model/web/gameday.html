Run Ledger

Starts from the posted total and moves it only for what the number does not already know. Anything the line has bought is subtracted before it counts.

The game from the odds board

The movement between these two is half the model. A number that has already come down has bought its own under — that part is spent.

Weather documented

Read the hourly row nearest first pitch, never the card headline. “8 mph wind blowing in West Sacramento” is a card whose own hourly row says out — that “in” is the preposition. Nothing under 8 mph counts; a cross wind is explicitly zero.

Plate umpire documented

His over/under record, not his runs per game — R/Gm is confounded by which parks he drew. Career numbers if you can get them. One season is about 30 plate games against a 335-game prior, so a 3-0 umpire topping a leaderboard is worth roughly +0.05 runs.

Money documented

Many small bets on the over against fewer large ones is the standard sharp tell. Needs a 20-point gap to register, and is capped flat — the direction is documented, the size is not.

Form provisional · trial

Both measured null on the 116-game log — head to head t = −0.40, team form t = −0.07. Built because they were asked for, sized small, and they cannot carry a call in Strict. Head to head refuses to read under three meetings.

Arms, recent provisional · trial

Five starts is about 27 innings against a 120-inning half-weight point, so under a fifth of the gap survives. A starter at 8.30 over his last five moves the total about a third of a run, not four.
