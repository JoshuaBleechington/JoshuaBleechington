Weather wind up to 1.5 · temp up to 0.35

RotoWire · park weather Swish · weather report Ballpark Pal

Read the hourly row nearest first pitch, never the card headline. “8 mph wind blowing in West Sacramento” is a card whose own hourly row says out — that “in” is the preposition, and reading it wrong flips the biggest number on this page. Under 8 mph counts for nothing. Across counts for nothing.

Park factor scales the wind

Statcast park factors ESPN park factors

100 is neutral. This does not get a term of its own — park factor is published, season-long and the same every night, so the posted total already has Coors in it and already has Petco in it. Adding it again is the double count that put the old model behind the naked line.

What it legitimately changes is what tonight’s wind is worth: 15 mph out where balls carry is not the same event as 15 out where they die on the track. So it multiplies the wind and nothing else, held inside ±20% — a 113 park lifts a 1.00-run wind to 1.13. With no wind reading it does nothing at all. Anything outside 70–130 is read as a typo and ignored.

Starting pitchers last 5 · trial

RotoWire · lineups & starters Covers · matchups

ERA over the last five starts and the innings behind it. Five starts is about 27 innings against a 120-inning half-weight point, so under a fifth of the gap from a 4.30 league ERA survives, then it is scaled by the five of nine innings a starter actually covers. A man at 8.30 over his last five moves the total about a third of a run, not four. Capped at 0.70 for the pair.

This is the one input here that has genuinely never been tested — the 116-game log only ever held season ERA. That is why it runs in Trial.

Bullpens era only · trial

FanGraphs · reliever leaders B-Ref · team pitching

Two numbers, no innings. The regression still happens — an unshrunk ERA gap is exactly the false precision this model exists to refuse — so a 110-inning workload is assumed, about a month of bullpen work, and 55% of the gap from a 4.30 league ERA survives. Then it is scaled by the roughly four of nine innings a pen covers, which is more than most people credit it with and is the half of the game the starter fields say nothing about. Capped at 0.60.

Enter a season bullpen ERA and it will be under-weighted rather than over-weighted: ~450 innings would earn 84% of the gap where this assumes 55%. A guess that errs toward not betting is the right way for a guess to be wrong.

This is bullpen form, not availability. Who threw last night and cannot go is the better idea and the one with a real claim to being unpriced, but it needs beat notes that are hard to get and impossible to check afterwards. Form is what a screenshot can carry.

Last 5 & head to head measured null · trial

Covers · matchups & H2H TeamRankings · runs per game

Enter the combined total of each side’s last five games, not their own runs. The average of the two is compared to tonight’s line and 15% of the gap is taken, capped at 0.60. Head to head takes 10%, capped at 0.40, and refuses to read under three meetings — letting a two-game head-to-head vote is the specific fault that cost four picks a confidence band in the old model.

Both were measured against the residual on the 116-game log and came back null: form t = −0.07, head to head t = −0.40. They are here because they were asked for, sized to match what they measured.

What counts as documented now. With the umpire off the page, the only items that can satisfy the grounded gate are the wind, the temperature and the money split. On a still night with the roof open and no split entered, Strict has nothing to stand on and every game reads PASS — that is the gate working, not the page failing. The wind is doing most of the work here; it is also the easiest thing on the list to look up.

Strict vs Trial. The four cards marked trial rest on coefficients nobody has validated, so in Strict they cannot satisfy the grounded gate and a game carried only by them reads PASS. Trial lets them carry a LEAN — never a BET, because a top-band call on unmeasured numbers is the false confidence this model exists to refuse. Log every game both ways against the same line; after thirty or forty the comparison answers whether they are worth keeping, which is the thing that was never done the first time these inputs were in a model.
