Staking & trust

How far to trust your numbers over the posted line. At 0.50 the projection meets the market halfway. Push toward 1.00 only after you've checked your inputs against closing lines; at 0.00 the model always agrees with the market and never bets.

Bets are sized with fractional Kelly and then hard-capped. The cap is what stops an overconfident projection from recommending a fifth of your bankroll.

Read this before you act on a number. The model knows season-long rates and the line you typed — not bullpen usage over the last three days, a scratched starter, or a rest day for a star. It has not been backtested against closing lines. Real edges in liquid markets run 1–3%; if you see 30%, an input is wrong, not the market. Sports betting involves risk, and no model guarantees a profit.
